01When and where is the tournament?+

Saturday 15 May 2027 at Stade Pelchat in Gif-sur-Yvette, France. Arrival times and the tournament entrance will be confirmed by the organisers.

02Who is this edition for?+

Touch In Paris 2027 is a mixed touch rugby tournament. Eligibility rules and team entry details should be confirmed with the organisers.

03How do I enter a team?+

Registration opens soon. A link to the Google Forms registration form will be published here. No account is needed on this website. Fees and available places will be confirmed by the organisers.

04Where can I find fixtures and results?+

The event section brings together tournament announcements. Detailed teams, fixtures and standings are available on the Competition page, with protected access.

05How do I collect my jersey?+

The collector page specifies collection at the tournament on Saturday 15 May 2027. Choose your style and size in the HelloAsso shop, where order conditions are provided.

06Accessibility needs or a specific request?+

Email info.touchinparis@gmail.com to plan your visit with the organisers. Event-specific accessibility details will be confirmed before the tournament.
